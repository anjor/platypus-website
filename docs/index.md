## FDE Team Building

We design the hiring system that identifies people who can succeed in FDE-style roles. This includes:

- Defining the traits and competencies that predict success at your company (not generic engineering skills)
- Designing an end-to-end interview process that tests for those specific traits
- Building interview kits: custom questions, technical challenges, scorecards
- Conducting interviews through our bench of Palantir-trained interviewers (Interviewing as a Service)
- Writing hiring theses: per-candidate documents that predict strengths, weaknesses, and ideal first projects
- Interviewer calibration and training

This is meaningfully different from generic engineering hiring. FDE candidates need a specific combination of technical depth, customer empathy, ownership mentality, and comfort with ambiguity that standard engineering interviews miss.

**Case studies:**

- [Comand AI](https://anjor.xyz/writing/2024/09/28/comand-ai-a-hiring-case-study/): Designed hiring for mission-driven FDE-style engineers
- [Northslope Technologies](https://anjor.xyz/writing/2024/11/20/case-study-building-a-hiring-process-for-northslope/): Built bespoke framework defining cultural traits vs role competencies
- [Prodigal Tech](https://www.prodigaltech.com/): Building their FDE organization from the ground up

**Pricing:** Hiring Blueprint from £10,000–£15,000 (one-time). Interviewing as a Service from £3,000/month.
