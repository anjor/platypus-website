# Engagements

Platypus has three lines of FDE work: The FDE Programme, advisory and interview design. All prices are in GBP and exclude VAT.

## The FDE Programme

An eight-week, remote programme that gets new FDEs ready to lead deployments. It is not a course. The AI labs teach the tools. The hard part is the craft of making a deployment work, and that grows through feedback on real work from people who have done it.

- **A two-day exercise.** Each pod gets a realistic client, not a specification. We play the client, and we know the answer, so feedback is sharp and every FDE faces the same test.
- **Six weeks of coaching on real work.** Each deployment team brings its live deployment to a coach for an hour a week. The coach asks who makes the call, which decision is being modelled and what changed for the user. Then they set one thing for next week.
- **A written verdict on each FDE.** In week 8, each FDE defends a fresh case to a panel. The verdict is ready to lead a deployment, ready with support, or not yet, with the evidence behind it. A short check follows 90 days later.

You also get a note on what in your company is blocking your FDEs, and the exercise, rubric and coach guide, licensed for internal use.

Add-ons include a case built from one of your own solved engagements, coaching beyond week 7 by the month, and moderation of repeat cohorts you run yourselves. In-person days are available, with travel at cost.

## Advisory

### FDE Readiness Assessment

A two-week assessment for a company deciding whether to build an FDE function, or trying to understand why an existing one is not working as expected.

We review the current plan and evidence, speak to the relevant leaders and practitioners, and produce a written Readout covering:

- what problem the field function needs to solve;
- the most important gaps or risks;
- our recommendation; and
- a practical 90-day plan.

**Price: £15,000.**

### Operating Model + Interview System Sprint

A fixed three-week engagement for a company ready to design or reset the function. It produces:

- an operating model covering team shape, ownership, interfaces and measurement;
- a hiring playbook with role profiles, interview design and calibration; and
- a clear description of the role for candidates, customers and the wider company.

**Price: £40,000.**

Longer-term implementation support is scoped after the initial engagement. It is not sold as a generic retainer before we understand the company.

## Interview design

If you already know the role, we can design how you interview for it, without a broader operating-model engagement.

**Interview process design: from £4,000 per role.** This includes the traits, interview structure, calibrated questions and a working session with the hiring team.

**Interviews: £600 each.** One of our calibrated interviewers runs the interview and sends written evidence within 48 hours.

We don't source or place candidates.
